# Surface: Deebo's College app shell and every screen

Scope: `frontend/src/App.jsx` and every screen it renders. Visitor mode: Operate. The user works through a profile, an interview, a ranked index, and a CSV export. The world serves the task.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A printed JoSAA cutoff almanac crossed with a terminal, not a dashboard of recommendation cards. The page is a ledger that finds your line in it. The category default it refuses is a centered hero card followed by a grid of college cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep ink navy ground, parchment text, terracotta axis, sage and sunkissed state ticks, accent blue actions, 1px hairlines, square corners, 20px grid on the profile screen, Source Serif 4 display, Commit Mono body and data, Departure Mono folios and axis labels. The signature is the Rank Rail: a log-scale rail with the student's rank fixed at center and each closing rank ticked left for reach or right for safe, with a text label.

STORY: A student enters rank, category, gender, home state and preferences. They understand which branches are eligible, answer a short interview, scan a dense but legible index, expand the evidence for a row, refine, and export a JoSAA-ready choice list. They never mistake a demo login for real authentication.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 12-column page under a 64px top bar. Left 7 columns: `01 / Profile` running head, a 64px serif line `Find where your rank lands.`, a short lede, then Rank as the largest control, a 3-up category/gender/state row, an Advanced-rank reveal, a preferences textarea, and the accent-blue `Build my index` action. Right 5 columns: a sticky specimen figure with corner marks, a log-scale Rank Rail from 1 to 300,000, terracotta and tidal markers, real catalogue counts in the caption, and a small legend. The grid texture is visible here only. No centered card, no hero image, no eyebrow directly above the heading.

FORM: code-led, pinned direction, no concept roll. The world is pinned by the user's words: "https://pi.dev/ use this style" and "dark only". Position on the ordered list: pinned. `seed: none (pinned) "https://pi.dev/ use this style"`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
